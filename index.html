02

MERCH — LET EVERYONE BUY THE SHIRT

Right now the only way to buy a Run It UP! shirt is to show up to the run and grab one out of a bag. If you're one of the 82,000 people following on Instagram — but you didn't make it out that Saturday — there's no way to buy anything.

The app adds a store. Anyone who likes Run It UP! can buy a shirt. Simple as that.

HOW CHECKOUT WORKS

  • Apple Pay & Google Pay built in — one tap with Face ID or fingerprint. No typing card numbers, no friction.
  • Payments powered by Stripe — same processor as Shopify, Amazon, Instacart. Billions of dollars run through it every year.
  • The Stripe account belongs to Run It UP! — every dollar lands directly in the org's bank. Nothing routes through me.
  • Members don't need an account to buy — one tap, shirt's on the way.

WHAT IT COSTS TO RUN

Shopify
$29–$299/mo + transaction fees + app add-ons
In-app store
$0/mo — just 2.9% + 30¢ per sale (Stripe's standard rate, same as any online store)

No monthly software fee. No separate website to build or maintain. Zero fixed cost — you only pay when something actually sells.

WHAT THE TEAM SEES

Every order emails Run It UP! instantly — buyer, shirt, size, pickup or ship. Orders also show in your Stripe dashboard (all-time revenue, unfulfilled orders, exports, etc). For v1, we'd launch with pickup at the next run — zero shipping logistics. Your team pulls the shirt from the bag with a printed list, hands it over Saturday. Shipping can add later when the demand justifies it.

BONUS — STORE DOUBLES AS SPONSOR REDEMPTION

When Propel runs a giveaway ("enter when you check in Saturday"), the winner's prize gets redeemed right here in the app store — free Propel bundle, free ASICS shoe voucher, 15% off at Champs. Same infrastructure. One checkout flow serves both merch AND sponsor activations.

THE BONUS PLAY

Special-edition drops tied to milestones. Hit a 4-week streak? The Streak Tee unlocks for you. Run the anniversary run? The commemorative tee is only buyable by the crew that was there. A way to make certain pieces feel earned — but the main point stays simple: people want to rep the crew, and right now most of them can't.

03

SPONSORS — CHARGE MORE BECAUSE THEY CAN FINALLY SEE WHAT THEY'RE PAYING FOR

Right now Propel, ASICS, and Champs pay Run It UP! because they trust the community is here. They can't actually see it. They send a check, you throw a run, they hope it worked.

The app changes the math — because now they can see it, measure it, and talk to the community directly.

Old way: Propel sponsors a run. Their logo is on a flyer. An Instagram post goes up for 24 hours and disappears. Propel has no idea who saw it, who tried their water, who bought Propel after. They're paying for a vibe.

New way: Propel pays to own a channel inside the app — #propel-hydration-tips — for 90 days. 800 members opt in. Propel posts tips, runs giveaways, talks to them every week. They get real numbers: who followed, who engaged, who ran the race, who entered the giveaway. That's a relationship, not a hope-it-worked flyer.

Same sponsor money. Way more value. Which means Run It UP! can charge more — because brands will pay more for something they can actually measure.

05.5 · FOR THE BOARD

A BOARD-READY ONE-PAGER.

Forward this section directly — every question a treasurer or board member will raise, answered.

Who owns the app?

Run It UP! owns the brand, the member data, and the App Store listing's public identity. Taylormade Creative owns the codebase and continues maintaining it under license.

Who owns the data?

Run It UP! — 100%. Every member record, every check-in, every DM. Exportable anytime. Data never leaves the org's Supabase account.

What if we end the relationship?

90-day transition window. Full data export (CSV + JSON). We help you stand up your next vendor or archive gracefully.

How is member privacy handled?

Row-level security at the database layer. Members can only see their own data. Blocked/reported users isolated. Apple/Google Sign-In means no password storage.

What's the total cost?

One-time setup + monthly maintenance. We walk through the numbers live — structured so it's predictable, budget-friendly for a 501(c)(3), and scales with actual usage.

What happens if Taylormade Creative disappears?

Source code held in escrow + full credential handoff on request. The app doesn't stop working. You hire another iOS dev and they pick up from there.
